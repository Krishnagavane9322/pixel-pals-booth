# ROVI AI Photo Booth -- Client Setup & Quick Start Guide

Welcome to your ROVI AI Photo Booth! This guide explains how to use your photo booth system for events with zero technical setup.

---

## ⚡ Option 1: 1-Click Launch (Recommended)

1. Double-click **`start-booth.bat`** (or **`start-booth-silent.vbs`**) on your Windows Desktop.
2. The system will automatically:
   - Start the background DNP DS-RX1 print engine.
   - Launch the Photo Booth Kiosk in full-screen mode (`https://pixel-pals-booth.vercel.app`).
3. Your Photo Booth is live and ready for guests!

---

## 🤖 Option 2: 100% Automatic Auto-Start on Boot (Zero Clicks!)

If you want the photo booth to open automatically whenever you power on the laptop:

1. Press **`Win + R`** on your keyboard to open the Run window.
2. Type **`shell:startup`** and press **Enter**.
3. Copy a shortcut of **`start-booth-silent.vbs`** into that Startup folder.
4. **Done!** Whenever you turn on the laptop, the photo booth and printer engine start automatically.

---

## 📱 Tablet / Remote Setup for Guests

1. Open Safari or Chrome on your iPad/Tablet and go to:
   ```
   https://pixel-pals-booth.vercel.app/tablet
   ```
   *(Or scan the QR code displayed on the Laptop's Admin screen)*.
2. Guests tap **TOUCH TO START** on the Tablet.
3. When guests tap **Print Photo**, prints are spooled directly to your **DNP DS-RX1 printer** silently in <1 second!

---

## 🖨️ Printer Check

- Make sure your **DNP DS-RX1 printer** is plugged into your laptop via USB and powered ON before launching the booth.
